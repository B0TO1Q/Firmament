Selah: church channel strip. Zero added delay, for the room and the livestream mix.

**Downloads**
- **Mac** (VST3 + AU + standalone app): `Selah-mac.zip`. Unzip, right-click `Install.command`, choose Open, then restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
- **Windows** (VST3 + standalone app): `Selah-windows.zip`. Unzip and copy `Selah.vst3` into `C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3`, then rescan plugins.

**What's in it**
- Nine sources: Speaker Mic, Lead Vocal, Choir, Acoustic Guitar, Electric Guitar, Bass, Keys, Drums, Full Mix
- Six knobs: Clean Up, Warmth, Clarity, Even Out, Space (Firmament reverb), Volume
- 7 / 14 / 21-band graphic EQ, before or after the channel processing
- Mono / Stereo switch, safety limiter with TOO LOUD light, Advanced drawer

**Checked**
- Passes pluginval at strictness 8 (Mac VST3 + AU, Windows VST3)
- Zero latency, transparent with every knob down
